# Palimpsest demo narration

Recorded on the running local application. All sample data is synthetic. No real personal data.

## 01 / The case desk

Palimpsest is a document investigation desk. A palimpsest is a manuscript rewritten over traces of earlier text. Here, we look for similar traces and give reviewers the evidence to make their own decision.

## 02 / Meena

Meena's payslip is slightly blurry. Her bank statement uses an initial, and the salary arrives a few days later. The app links the pay cycle, explains the name variant, and gives the case low concern.

## 03 / Ravi and the red box

Ravi's payslip shows eighty-eight thousand rupees in net pay. The amount uses different typography from its neighbors. This finding takes us directly to the affected region on the original page.

## 04 / Peel the Layer

The PDF kept its earlier revision. Peel the Layer recovers forty-eight thousand rupees and places it beside the current amount. A legitimate payroll correction could leave the same history.

## 05 / Compare sources

Gross pay minus deductions also gives forty-eight thousand rupees. That matches the bank credit for the pay cycle. The payslip and bank statement also have near-identical signature regions. A shared approved stamp could explain them.

## 06 / Human decision

The concern breakdown shows the contribution of each finding. We request the issuer's final payroll record and the matching bank credit. Palimpsest prepares a polite letter, and the reviewer records the decision.

## 07 / Dossier and chain

The dossier contains the source hashes, evidence, limits and reviewer decision. We download it, then verify the audit chain. Each entry commits to the event and the previous hash.

## 08 / Embedded instruction

This address proof contains white text asking an automated reviewer to approve it. Palimpsest surfaces that text as evidence. The review stays pending until a person takes action.

## 09 / Cannot determine

This scan is too degraded to read. The app returns cannot determine, explains the readability issue, and asks for a higher-quality source.

## 10 / Measured limits

On twenty synthetic cases, nine changed cases were flagged and one was missed. No genuine cases were flagged in that set. In the scan sweep, rotation caused one false alarm, and four blurred inputs could not be read reliably.

## 11 / Close

Every document has a second layer. Palimpsest helps a reviewer trace what changed, weigh plausible explanations and decide what to verify next. Built by hannielvinu for Hack The Core.
